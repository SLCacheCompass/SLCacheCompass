Cache Compass Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit the Cache Compass website, purchase a license, activate or use Cache Compass, request support, or otherwise interact with Cache Compass.

1. Information We May Collect

Purchase and customer information. Depending on the purchase method, we may process your name or contact information, email address, order and transaction identifiers, product or license tier, payment status, and refund, dispute, or chargeback status. Payment-card information is processed by the applicable payment provider; Cache Compass does not need to store your full payment-card number.

License information. We may process license identifiers, license status, licensed avatar capacity, activation and entitlement records, Second Life avatar names and/or UUIDs associated with licensed slots, current and historical avatar assignments, upgrades, additional capacity, complimentary licenses, and administrative adjustments.

Technical and security information. We may process limited technical information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and prevent abuse of Cache Compass, including application version information, timestamps, licensing requests, error information, and security-related events.

Support information. If you contact Cache Compass, we may retain the messages and information you provide so we can respond, troubleshoot problems, document resolutions, and prevent repeated abuse or fraud.

2. Information Cache Compass Does Not Need

Cache Compass does not require your Second Life password. Do not provide your Second Life password to Cache Compass. Cache Compass is designed to work alongside your existing Firestorm environment rather than requiring you to surrender your Second Life login credentials.

3. How Information Is Used

We may use information to provide and activate licenses; associate licensed avatars with your entitlement; administer upgrades and capacity; process purchases, refunds, and payment disputes; maintain historical licensing records; prevent fraud, piracy, unauthorized sharing, and license circumvention; provide support; diagnose technical problems; maintain security; improve Cache Compass; comply with legal obligations; and establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

We do not sell personal information for money.

4. Inventory Data

Cache Compass interacts with locally available Firestorm-related inventory information in order to perform inventory-management functions. Cache Compass is not intended to create a commercial database of your Second Life inventory or sell your inventory information to advertisers. Information necessary for licensing, security, troubleshooting, or support may be transmitted to Cache Compass services where required for those functions.

5. Payment Processors and Service Providers

Cache Compass may use third-party service providers for payment processing, website hosting, licensing infrastructure, database hosting, email delivery, security, error reporting, and other operational functions. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own legal and contractual obligations.

6. Legal Disclosures

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process; investigate fraud or unlawful conduct; protect Cache Compass, its users, or others from security threats; enforce our Terms; or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected. Licensing, purchase, payment, fraud-prevention, and avatar-assignment history may be retained after an active assignment or license changes because those records may be necessary to document ownership, licensing history, prior assignments, chargebacks, disputes, support issues, or security events. Where applicable law requires deletion or limits retention, Cache Compass will comply with those requirements.

8. Data Security

Cache Compass uses reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights concerning personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information regarding how your data is processed. Requests may be submitted through the official Cache Compass contact method. We may need to verify your identity. Some information may lawfully be retained when necessary for transactions, fraud prevention, security, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or establishment or defense of legal claims.

10. Children

Cache Compass is a software utility intended for users capable of entering into a legally binding purchase and software license agreement. Cache Compass is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Third-Party Platforms

Cache Compass is independent of Linden Research, Inc., Second Life, Firestorm, and The Phoenix Firestorm Project. Information those services collect is governed by their respective privacy policies, not this Privacy Policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Cache Compass or applicable law changes. The effective date shown at the top identifies the current version.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be submitted through the official contact method listed on the Cache Compass website.
