HOW IT WORKS

Big inventory. Small, deliberate moves.

Cache Compass does the digging first. Live Second Life actions happen only after you’ve reviewed what it found.

1

Refresh in Firestorm

Let your inventory finish loading, then log out and close Firestorm so its local inventory data is current.

2

Find the problem

Search by keyword or choose the cleanup categories you want Cache Compass to inspect. Nothing moves during scanning.

3

Review everything

Inspect the proposed cleanup list and uncheck anything you want to keep before a live action is allowed.

4

Run the Safety Test

Cache Compass opens a temporary Second Life session, moves one reviewed item to Trash, verifies the move, and disconnects.

5

Check it yourself

Open Firestorm, inspect Trash, and confirm the Safety Test moved exactly what you expected before Move Files unlocks.

6

Move approved items

Cache Compass rechecks approved items, moves them to Second Life Trash, and disconnects. It never empties Trash for you.

✓

YOUR INVENTORY. YOUR CALL.

Cache Compass doesn’t decide what you don’t need. You do.

Scans don’t move inventory. Cleanup candidates are shown to you first. No-copy inventory, links, Current Outfit references, Marketplace folders, Trash, protected system folders, and your protected folders receive additional safeguards.

Before a full cleanup, Safety Test moves one reviewed item so you can verify the process yourself. Cache Compass can restore supported journaled moves to their original folders while they remain in Trash — and it never empties Trash.

A Second Life password is requested only for a temporary live session used by Safety Test, Move Files, or Restore Items. It is not saved to disk, written to Cache Compass reports or logs, or retained after the login attempt.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The questions we’d ask before trusting an inventory tool.

Can Cache Compass search my inventory?

Yes. Cache Compass can search inventory by keyword and narrow results by supported inventory types. Search-only workflows are read-only and do not move inventory.

What is keyword cleanup?

Keyword cleanup lets you use a term such as DEMO, a body-size name, store name, or product name to build a reviewable cleanup list. You review the matches before any approved live action.

Do I log into Second Life through Cache Compass?

A Second Life login is requested when Cache Compass needs to perform a live inventory action such as Safety Test, Move Files, or Restore Items. The password is used for that session and is not stored or retained by Cache Compass.

Is Cache Compass another Second Life viewer?

No. You continue to use Firestorm normally. For approved live inventory actions, Cache Compass temporarily connects to Second Life as an inventory-focused third-party client and disconnects when the operation is complete.

What duplicate types does Cache Compass support?

The current cleanup set includes landmarks, notecards, objects, folders, empty folders, calling cards, sounds, animations, and gestures.

What happens to no-copy items?

Cache Compass is designed to treat no-copy inventory conservatively and protect it from duplicate cleanup workflows.

Is Cache Compass affiliated with Linden Lab or Firestorm?

No. Cache Compass is an independent third-party companion utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Linden Lab or the Firestorm Viewer project.
