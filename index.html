SEARCH ONLY

Search it like you remember it.

Firestorm search is useful when you already know enough of the item’s exact name. But real inventories are messy: creators use punctuation, decorative symbols, abbreviations, body-size suffixes, version numbers, and folder names that may not match the words you remember. When you only remember pieces of the name, ordinary viewer search can turn into trial-and-error.

You do not need to know the exact name the creator used. For example, if you remember that you own a Reborn shirt but cannot remember whether the creator named it Reborn Knit Shirt - Cream, [STORE] Knit Top (Reborn), or buried it inside a folder called Reborn / Tops, searching reborn shirt can use those remembered words and folder-path context to narrow the results instead of making you guess one exact inventory name.

Maybe you remember the store name. Maybe the body size. Maybe only one word from the product. Search Only looks through the current Firestorm inventory snapshot using keywords and supported inventory types.

It is read-only. Search Only does not move files, touch Trash, or ask for your Second Life login.

CLEANUP

Stop deleting the same leftovers one folder at a time.

Landmarks. Notecards. Unpacking scripts. Empty folders. Calling cards. Duplicates. They’re useful when you need them; years later, they can be thousands of tiny jobs waiting for you.

Choose duplicate-cleanup categories or use a keyword to build a targeted review list. Want to look for old body sizes? Demos? A store you haven’t worn since three logos ago? Start with the word you remember.

Nothing is moved while Cache Compass builds the list. Protected inventory stays out of the cleanup plan. You review the pile; Cache Compass saves you from hunting for it.

REVIEW FIRST

One keeper stays. You choose the extras.

For duplicate sets, Cache Compass reserves one keeper before Review. The extras are what you see. Nothing shown is automatically approved: select only the rows you actually want moved.

When you click Move Files, keep Cache Compass open and leave Firestorm in the background. Cache Compass targets the inventory UUIDs you approved and moves those items through the Firestorm session you already have open.

HOW IT WORKS

Firestorm stays open. Cache Compass stays in its lane.

No second viewer login, no password handoff, and no foreground keyboard automation. Cache Compass works alongside the Firestorm session you already opened.

1

Log in with Firestorm

Open Firestorm, log into Second Life, open Inventory, and let it finish loading. Keep Firestorm open while you use Cache Compass.

2

Start Here

Cache Compass detects the supported Firestorm session and binds to the personal inventory of the avatar currently signed in.

3

Search or build a review

Search Only stays read-only. Cleanup can surface duplicate candidates, empty folders, or keyword matches for review.

4

Review the extras

Review opens without blindly approving everything. Check only the items you want moved. For duplicate sets, one keeper has already been reserved.

5

Move Files

Keep Cache Compass open and leave Firestorm in the background. Cache Compass targets approved inventory UUIDs through Firestorm’s local window channel and moves those approved items.

6

Check Trash in Firestorm

Moved items go to Second Life Trash for another review. Cache Compass never empties Trash, so the final permanent-delete decision stays with you.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your SL password is Firestorm’s business. Not ours.

Cache Compass never asks for, receives, transmits, or stores your Second Life username, password, or MFA code. Firestorm remains the software authenticated to Second Life.

Cache Compass reads a temporary, read-only snapshot of Firestorm’s local inventory data for indexing, cleanup planning, and review. It does not write to Firestorm’s cache or settings. Foreground keyboard automation is disabled; approved moves use the supported background Firestorm bridge and target inventory by UUID.

Cleanup also treats no-copy inventory, inventory links, Current Outfit references, Trash, Marketplace folders, protected system folders, and your own Protected Folders conservatively.

FAQ

The questions we’d ask before letting anything near our inventory.

Does Cache Compass need my Second Life password?

No. Cache Compass does not ask for, receive, transmit, or store your Second Life username, password, or MFA code. You log into Second Life through Firestorm as usual.

Does Firestorm need to stay open?

Yes. Open Firestorm, let Inventory finish loading, and keep Firestorm open while using Cache Compass. During Move Files, leave Firestorm in the background and keep the Cache Compass window open until the move finishes.

Can I search without changing anything?

Yes. Search Only is read-only. It searches the current local Firestorm inventory snapshot and does not move files.

What happens to duplicate sets?

Cache Compass reserves one keeper from each duplicate set before the extras reach Review. Review shows the extra candidates, and you decide which of those should move.

Where do approved files go?

Cache Compass moves approved reviewed items to Second Life Trash through the Firestorm session you already have open. Cache Compass never empties Trash.

What inventory does Cache Compass protect?

The current safeguards include no-copy inventory, inventory links, Current Outfit references, Trash, Marketplace folders, protected system folders, and folders you add to Protected Folders.

Can I use it on an alt?

Cache Compass works with the avatar currently signed in to the supported Firestorm session. Switch avatars in Firestorm first; Cache Compass does not use a separate manual account picker.

Is Cache Compass affiliated with Linden Lab or Firestorm?

No. Cache Compass is an independent third-party companion utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Linden Lab or the Firestorm Viewer project.
