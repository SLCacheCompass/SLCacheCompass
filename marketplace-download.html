SECOND LIFE MARKETPLACE PURCHASE

Download Cache Compass

Marketplace customers do not need a Cache Compass license key. Enter the Marketplace order or transaction number from your purchase history to prepare a secure installer for the avatar that received the license.

Marketplace download

Use the order or transaction number shown in Second Life Marketplace purchase history. The resulting installer carries a signed activation receipt bound to the avatar that received this purchase.

Signed purchase-specific activation receipt Temporary download link No Mainstore visit required

Activation

Your avatar is the entitlement.

After installation, log into Firestorm with the avatar that received the Marketplace license and open Cache Compass. The signed receipt is bound to that entitlement and cannot activate a different avatar.

Windows installation note

Windows may show a security warning.

The current release may display an Unknown Publisher or SmartScreen warning during installation. This does not change the licensing requirement.
