Cache Compass Refund Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Because Cache Compass is digitally delivered licensed software whose value can be received immediately after activation and use, purchases are generally final once the license has been delivered or activated.

Refunds We Will Review

Cache Compass will review refund requests involving:

  • duplicate or accidental duplicate charges;
  • payment taken when a license was not successfully provisioned;
  • a verified technical failure attributable to Cache Compass that prevents the purchased license from being activated or used and that Cache Compass cannot reasonably correct;
  • an incorrect charge or license tier caused by a Cache Compass purchasing-system error; or
  • circumstances in which applicable law requires a refund.

Situations That Normally Do Not Qualify

A refund normally will not be provided because:

  • you changed your mind after purchase;
  • you no longer want to organize your inventory;
  • you expected Cache Compass to make subjective inventory decisions for you;
  • you deleted or moved inventory after approving an operation;
  • a third-party product or platform outside Cache Compass’s control changed;
  • your computer does not meet stated requirements;
  • you purchased capacity that you later decided you did not need; or
  • your license was suspended or revoked because of fraud, chargeback, piracy, unauthorized redistribution, deliberate license circumvention, or another material violation of the Software License Agreement.

Mandatory Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy limits refund, cancellation, warranty, or consumer rights that applicable law does not allow Cache Compass to waive.

How to Request Review

Submit refund requests through the official Cache Compass support method with enough information to identify the purchase and describe the issue.
